# Summer Glow Launch Campaign

## Campaign Goal

Establish Aura Glow's hydrating serum as the summer skincare essential, highlighting sun recovery and deep hydration.

## Target Audience

Skincare enthusiasts aged 20-45 looking for light, non-greasy summer skin barrier support.

## Ad Creative Copy

"Sun-kissed but skin-saved. Discover the restorative power of Aura Glow Serum. 10% Niacinamide + Hyaluronic Acid blocks dehydration and restores radiance after sun exposure."

## Compliance Notes

All ad copy must avoid claiming SPF protection or clinical sun damage reversal. Highlight "hydration recovery" instead.
